Cheap Watts is run by Jon Russell. The quickest way to reach me is by email: **[jon@jondoesflow.com](mailto:jon@jondoesflow.com)**.

Get in touch about:

- **A mistake or an out-of-date figure.** Tell me the page and what looks wrong, and I'll check it against the source.
- **Brands, retailers and affiliate programmes.** I'm happy to talk about products that genuinely help people cut their energy bills. Cheap Watts lists products on merit and shows what they cost to run; see the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
- **Councils, charities and housing associations.** You can [add Cheap Watts to your website for free](/widget-for-your-website/), and I'm glad to help.
- **Press.** I can work out running costs or tariff comparisons for a story.

You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jondoesflow). For how Cheap Watts handles your data, see the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
