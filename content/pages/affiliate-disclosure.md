Cheap Watts is free to use. To help pay for it, some pages include affiliate links. This page explains what that means.

## What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link takes you to a retailer's website. If you buy something after clicking it, the retailer may pay Cheap Watts a commission. You pay the same price as you would going to the retailer directly.

## Who we work with

- **Awin.** Cheap Watts is a publisher on the Awin affiliate network. When a retailer's programme has approved Cheap Watts, links to that retailer go through Awin.
- **Amazon.** Cheap Watts is a member of the Amazon Associates programme. As an Amazon Associate, Cheap Watts earns from qualifying purchases.

Links to a retailer that hasn't approved Cheap Watts go straight to the retailer and earn nothing.

## How the links are labelled

- Pages with affiliate links say so near the top, or right beside the links, before you reach them.
- In the gift guide, links that earn a commission are marked "affiliate link".
- Affiliate links are marked for search engines as sponsored, and open in a new tab.

## What affiliate links never change

- The tools' results. The cheapest time, a tariff comparison and a running cost are worked out from published prices and are the same whether or not a page has a link.
- Which products we list. Products are chosen because they help with the thing the page is about. No brand pays to be listed, and we don't take payment for reviews.
- The running costs we show. They are worked out from each product's own power rating and the Ofgem price cap, with the assumptions written out.

## Cookies

Cheap Watts sets no cookies of its own for affiliate links. When you click one, Awin, Amazon or the retailer may set cookies to record that you came from Cheap Watts, under their own privacy policies. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for more.
