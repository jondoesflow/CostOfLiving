Cheap Watts is a free website that helps UK households pay less for electricity. It is made in the UK by Jon Russell ([JonDoesFlow](https://www.jondoesflow.com/)).

## What it does

- **[The cheapest time to run your appliances](/).** Live half-hourly prices for your tariff and region, with the cheapest and greenest time to run the washing machine, the dishwasher, the car charger and more.
- **[Plan my evening](/plan-my-evening/).** Fits several appliances into the cheapest hours.
- **[Gas or electric?](/gas-or-electric/)** Compares everyday jobs at today's gas and electricity prices.
- **[Solar and batteries](/solar-and-batteries/).** When to use, sell or store your solar power.
- **[Should I switch?](/should-i-switch/)** Works out what you'd have paid on other tariffs from your own smart meter usage.
- **[Running costs](/cost-to-run/)**, the **[gift guide](/gifts/)** and the **[blog](/blog/)**: plain-English guides with the sums shown.

There's no account, no sign-up and no email address to give. There is also a Cheap Watts iPhone app.

## Where the numbers come from

Prices come from the public price data that Octopus Energy, EDF and E.ON Next publish, and the greenest times from National Grid's carbon intensity forecast. Guides use Ofgem's price cap figures, linked and dated. Cheap Watts is not affiliated with any energy supplier, and no supplier pays to be featured.

## How it's paid for

Cheap Watts has no banner adverts or pop-ups. Some pages carry clearly labelled affiliate links to products that help with the thing you're looking at, such as a heated throw on the electric heater page. If you buy through one, Cheap Watts may earn a commission from the retailer, at no extra cost to you. The links never change a result or a running cost. The [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) explains how they work. You can also [support the site on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jondoesflow).
