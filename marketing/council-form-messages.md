# Council contact form messages

These six councils only take messages through a website form, so these can't be emailed.
Paste each one into the form named. Plain text, under 1,500 characters, so it fits most
forms. Each has the council's region set in the box code (checked with the Octopus
postcode lookup on the council's head office postcode). Add each one to
outreach-log.md when sent.

Subject, where the form asks for one: A free tool for your cost of living page

## Essex County Council

- Form: https://www.essex.gov.uk/form/feedback-form?reference=2305 (the household bills support page (feedback form on that page))
- Region in the box code: A (Eastern England)

```text
Hello,

I'm writing about your Household bills support page and the Cut your energy costs page it links to, which already list the Energy Saving Trust and Switch Together. Please could this be passed to whoever looks after it.

I've built a free website, Cheap Watts (https://cheapwatts.co.uk), that shows residents what their appliances cost to run, the cheapest time to run them on their tariff, and whether a different tariff would save them money. It's free, with no sign-up, works on any phone and is built to the WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standard. It has no banner adverts or pop-ups; a few results link to products on Amazon as clearly labelled affiliate links.

You could add a link, or a free box that shows today's running costs in Eastern England. The box itself has no cookies, adverts or affiliate links. The code, with your region set, is:

<iframe src="https://cheapwatts.co.uk/widget/?r=A" title="Cheap Watts: what does it cost to run?" width="100%" height="460" style="border:0;max-width:480px" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Preview and instructions: https://cheapwatts.co.uk/widget-for-your-website/

Thank you,
Jon Russell
jon@jondoesflow.com
```
