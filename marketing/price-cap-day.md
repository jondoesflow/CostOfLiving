Ofgem announces the energy price cap for 1 January to 31 March 2027 on
25 November 2026 (source: Ofgem, as cited in the price cap explainer post). That
morning, energy bills are the news story, and editors and readers want a
practical angle. This pack has everything ready except Ofgem's figures.

Rule for everything below: fill the [square brackets] only from Ofgem's own
announcement (ofgem.gov.uk). Not from forecasts, not from press reports. Date

  in England, paid through council tax.
