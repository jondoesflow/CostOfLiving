# Partner case study

Use this when an organisation links to Cheap Watts or embeds the box. A short,
true story about one real partner makes the next outreach email more
convincing than any feature list.

Rules:

- Ask first. Don't name an organisation in public until they've said yes in
  writing, and send them the draft before it goes up.
- Use only what they tell you, what's on their own page, and figures from Umami.
  Never invent a quote: if they give one, use it word for word.
- Date every figure.

## 1. Permission email (send as a reply in the existing thread)

> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for adding Cheap Watts to [page name]. [Only if Umami shows visits
> from their site: "I can already see visits coming through from your page."
> Otherwise: "I've checked and it's working nicely on your page."]
>
> Would you be happy for me to write a short piece about it for the Cheap Watts
> blog and LinkedIn? It would say what [Organisation] does, why you added it,
> and how it's being used. I'd send you the draft before anything goes up, and
> I'd only include a quote if you give me one.
>
> Two quick questions, if you're happy to answer them:
>
> 1. What made you add it?
> 2. Is there anything your [advisers / tenants / residents] have said about it?
>
> Thanks again,
> Jon

## 2. Blog post brief (for Claude, once they've said yes)

> Write a short blog post (400 to 600 words): "How [Organisation] helps
> [residents / tenants] with energy costs using Cheap Watts". Use only: their
> reply in Outlook, their own web page that links to or embeds Cheap Watts, and
> Umami figures for visits from their site (utm_source=widget referrers, or
> their domain in Referrers), with dates. Cover who they are and who they help,
> where Cheap Watts sits on their site, what people use it for, and one practical
> tip for other organisations thinking of adding it, linking to
> /widget-for-your-website/. Quote them only word for word. Follow
> content/blog/README.md and the voice skill. Raise it as a pull request titled
> "Blog post for approval: ...", and email the draft text to the partner for
> sign-off before Jon merges.

## 3. LinkedIn post (after the blog post is live)

No markdown, and run the voice check with --linkedin.

> [Organisation] has added Cheap Watts to [page name], so [residents /
> tenants] can see what their appliances cost to run at today's prices, and
> the cheapest time to run them.
>
> [One true detail from the case study: why they added it, or how it's used.]
>
> If you run a cost of living or energy advice page, the box is free, has no
> cookies, adverts or affiliate links, and takes one line of code:
> https://cheapwatts.co.uk/widget-for-your-website/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=case-study
>
> Thank you to [named contact, only with their permission] for [what they did].
