# Outreach contacts: councils and advice organisations

Who to ask about listing Cheap Watts (https://cheapwatts.co.uk) on their cost of living or energy advice pages. All details checked on 2026-10-29. Every email or form below was seen on the organisation's own website on that date, with the source page given next to it. Anything I could not open myself is marked "not verified".

## How to use this

- Send five a week, not the whole list at once, so you can follow up properly and adjust the email as replies come in.
- Personalise the first line by mentioning their actual page (for example "I was reading your Help with energy page, which already links to Take Charge...") so it is clearly not a mass mailing.
- Log every send and reply (date, who, outcome, page URL if they add you) in a simple sheet, and chase once after two weeks.

## National and UK-wide organisations

### Citizens Advice

- Relevant to us: the biggest source of energy advice for households (consumer service, energy pages, local offices that run their own cost of living resource lists). Many councils above link to it.
- Contact route: there is no partnerships email. The media team takes journalists only: press.office@citizensadvice.org.uk, 03000 231 080 (source: https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/information/contact-our-media-team/). For a listing request, the better route is local offices, found by postcode at https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/nearby/ (linked from https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/). Start with offices that run energy projects, such as Preston (energysupport@prestoncitizensadvice.org.uk, listed on Lancashire County Council's energy page above).

### National Energy Action (NEA)

- Relevant to us: the national fuel poverty charity. Runs an energy advice helpline and trains frontline workers. Birmingham links its "get help" page.
- Contact route: press and media, Anna Cook (Head of Communications and Campaigns) anna.cook@nea.org.uk, or Nick Palmer (Press and Media Officer) nick.palmer@nea.org.uk. Head office 0191 261 5677. Source: https://www.nea.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/ . There is also a "Consultancy and partnerships" page: https://www.nea.org.uk/support-us/consultancy-and-partnerships/

### Centre for Sustainable Energy (CSE)

- Relevant to us: Bristol charity giving free home energy advice across the South West, and publishing energy advice guides.
- Contact route: general info@cse.org.uk (0117 934 1400); media natashac@cse.org.uk. Source: https://www.cse.org.uk/contact-us/ . (home.energy@cse.org.uk on the same page is the household advice line, so not for this.)

### Energy Saving Trust

- Relevant to us: runs energy saving advice and tools. Already linked by Essex, Hertfordshire and Devon.
- Contact route: not verified. energysavingtrust.org.uk blocked every fetch, and the search results hid the press and partnerships addresses. Contact page to open in a browser: https://energysavingtrust.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/

### Turn2us

- Relevant to us: national poverty charity with a benefits calculator, grants search and energy scheme information pages (Devon links one).
- Contact route: there is no general partnerships email. Press office media@turn2us.org.uk (source: https://www.turn2us.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/media-enquiry). Contact options page: https://www.turn2us.org.uk/about-us/contact-us

### Money and Pensions Service (MoneyHelper)

- Relevant to us: runs MoneyHelper, which has cost of living pages that Hertfordshire and Nottinghamshire link to. Also has a partnerships team that lets organisations reuse its content.
- Contact route: not verified. maps.org.uk and moneyhelper.org.uk blocked every fetch. Search snippets from maps.org.uk showed contact@maps.org.uk (general) and media@maps.org.uk (press office), and the MoneyHelper partnerships page at https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/about-us/partnerships/overview . Check these in a browser before using them.

### StepChange

- Relevant to us: debt charity whose clients are often behind on energy bills. Nottinghamshire links it.
- Contact route: press@stepchange.org, 0207 391 4598, journalists only (source: https://www.stepchange.org/media-centre.aspx). The "Partner with us" page (https://www.stepchange.org/about-us/partner-with-us.aspx) shows no email or form. Low priority.

### National Housing Federation (NHF)

- Relevant to us: trade body for housing associations, whose tenancy sustainment and money advice teams share tools with residents. Good route to many landlords at once.
- Contact route: general info@housing.org.uk (020 7067 1010); press team prteam@housing.org.uk (020 7067 1146). Source: https://www.housing.org.uk/about-us/contact-us/ . Partnerships information: https://www.housing.org.uk/partners/work-with-us/
